What documents do I need?

A driving licence held for at least two years and an ID. A foreign licence is accepted for a tourist stay.

How does the deposit work?

A card pre-authorisation at key handover, released when the car is returned.

Can I drive on dirt tracks?

The Atlas SUV is made for easy tracks. The compact stays on paved roads.

What if my flight is late?

Give us your flight number: the person delivering the car follows the actual arrival time.
