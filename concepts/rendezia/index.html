    Questions

    Do patients need an account?

    No. A name, a phone number and the reason for the visit are enough to book.

    Can I import my current calendar?

    Yes, from a file or from another online calendar. The team helps you set up.

    Where is the data stored?

    In this concept, on servers in Europe, with a host certified for health data.

    What if a patient does not show up?

    Mark the visit as missed in one click. You can then ask that patient for a deposit on future bookings.
