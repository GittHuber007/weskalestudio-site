From the Emmys

Our high-end diamond jewelry has been worn on red carpets around the world. A few moments from the archive.

  • Academy Awards, 2005

    Cate Blanchett

    Accepting the Oscar for Best Supporting Actress, wearing J. Birnbach handmade 18K yellow gold natural fancy yellow diamond drop earrings.

  • Academy Awards, 2005

    Winning the Oscar for Best Actress, wearing our classic handmade platinum leverback diamond drop earrings.
