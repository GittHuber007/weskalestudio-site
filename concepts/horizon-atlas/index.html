Transmission

Behind every success, a teacher who believed in it first.

A school is first of all people. Teachers who know each pupil, notice a doubt before it becomes a difficulty, and ask a little more of those who can give it.

The path

Fifteen years to grow.

  1. Ages 3 to 5

    Kindergarten

    First times: holding a pencil, telling a story, counting the olive trees in the courtyard.

  2. Ages 6 to 10

    Primary

    Reading, writing, reasoning. Solid foundations, built with method and patience.

  3. Ages 11 to 14

    Middle school

    The age of big questions. Laboratory, debate, group projects: learning to argue and to cooperate.

  4. Ages 15 to 17

    High school

    Specialities, guidance and the baccalaureate, with personal support towards higher education, in Morocco or abroad.
