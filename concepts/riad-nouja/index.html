The house

Riad Nouja is a four-room guesthouse at the end of a quiet lane in the medina. Guests come for the light in the courtyard, breakfast on the roof terrace and the silence, a few minutes' walk from the souks.

Cedar

01 · The door

Carved cedar

Carved by hand in cedar, the door carries a single brass plate. It is the only sign of the house in the lane.

Zellige

02 · The fountain

Hand-cut zellige

Each tile is cut with a hammer, then set by hand, pattern by pattern, around a white stone basin.

Orange

03 · The garden

The courtyard orange tree

Planted at the foot of the galleries, it gives shade in the afternoon. Its fruit ends up freshly pressed at breakfast.

Brass

04 · The lounge

Brass lanterns

Benches, rugs and lanterns: the courtyard is the living room, from morning tea to the last light of the evening.

All four rooms open onto the courtyard galleries.
