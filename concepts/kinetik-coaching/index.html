The approach

Slow the movement down.
Speed follows.

Every session starts at walking pace: we film, we watch, we correct. Load and speed come once the movement is clean.

The method

Three steps.
None left to chance.

  1. 01

    The assessment

    One hour to measure mobility, base strength and past injuries. We set a goal with a date.

  2. 02

    The plan

    Four-week blocks, with loads written down and the progression planned in advance.

  3. 03

    The follow-up

    A check-in every week, with adjustments for sleep, fatigue and work.
