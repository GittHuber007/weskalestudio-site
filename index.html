workflows and AI agents

Your processes run. Your teams move forward.

What is a workflow?

Picture a row of dominoes. Tip the first one and all the others follow, on their own, in the same order. A workflow does the same with your work: one action automatically triggers everything that comes next.

without a workflow
A client books a seminar. Someone records the booking, a colleague prepares the contract, another sends the invoice, then the on-site team has to be told. Four tasks, four chances to forget something.
with a workflow
The client books, and everything else happens on its own, in seconds.

What is an AI agent?

Picture an assistant that never sleeps. It reads your messages, understands what is being asked and knows what to do next. When a decision is yours to make, it brings it to you.

without an AI agent
At 11 p.m., a client writes: “We'll be 40 after all, not 25.” The message waits until morning, then someone has to check the rooms and redo the quote.
with an AI agent
The assistant reads the message, finds an available room and prepares the new quote. In the morning, your manager only has to approve it.

A workflow always follows the same path. An AI agent understands the situation and adapts.

We deploy workflows and AI agents connected to your tools to take on low-value tasks: qualification, follow-ups, sorting and data updates. Your teams step in where their judgment makes the difference.
